# BLANC — information needed before launch

The demo was built from the BLANC Instagram profile. Instagram showed only its login wall to automated access, so the research relied on the **screenshots supplied on 16 Nov 2026**: the profile logo, the *Facial Price List*, the *HIFU Body Pricelist*, the *Black Friday offers* post, and lash / brow photos.

Nothing below has been invented. Everything that wasn't visible is either a clearly-marked placeholder or listed here.

## Taken from Instagram (please check)

* **Branding:** chrome “BLANC.” logo on black with glitter; tagline “Skin | Aesthetics | Wellbeing” (an older post says “Beauty · Aesthetics · Wellness”); black, champagne-gold and cream colours; serif + script typography.
* **Prices:** Medical Microdermabrasion £45 / £70 / £35 · Let's Glow £55 / £80 · Needling £60 / £85 / Needling + Dermaplaning + Light Peel £100 / + Skin Booster £130 / + Under Eye Booster £150 · Morpheus8 £150 / £99 / £99 / Face, Neck & Décolletage £200 / Course of 3 £390 · HIFU Body single sessions £120–£270 by area (block of 6 = pay for 5).
* **BLANC's own wording** used for HIFU Body (“pain free, advanced technology…”, 3–6 sessions, 4–6 weeks apart) and the Pelvic Chair.

## Please confirm or supply

1. **Full treatment names** where the price-list screenshot was cut off (flagged “To confirm” on each treatment in the dashboard), e.g. which £99 Morpheus8 line is face and which is neck.
2. **Missing prices:** HIFU Face & Neck, Dermaplaning, Skin Boosters, Pelvic Chair, Lashes & Brows. These are listed on the site as “price to be confirmed” and can't be booked online yet.
3. **Durations** for every treatment (all current values are estimates).
4. **Deposits** per treatment: fixed £, percentage, or none. The demo uses a mix to show all three options.
5. **Booking policies:** deposit, cancellation notice, rescheduling, late arrivals, no-shows, refunds.
6. **Bank details** for deposits (account name, sort code, account number) and how long clients have to pay (demo: 24 hours).
7. **Opening hours and breaks** (demo: Tue–Sat placeholder hours), notice period, how far ahead clients can book, and the gap needed between clients.
8. **Contact details:** address, phone, email, Google Maps link.
9. **About:** the brand story, the therapist's name, qualifications, training and insurance.
10. **Photos:** high-resolution originals of the work, the treatment room and the owner. The demo uses small crops from the screenshots.
11. **Testimonials:** only genuine reviews, with the client's permission. None are shown in the demo.
12. **Treatment descriptions, preparation and aftercare** wording (the current text is a draft).
13. **Email address** for owner notifications, and a domain for sending emails.
14. How **course bookings** (e.g. Morpheus8 ×3, HIFU blocks of 6) and **gift vouchers** should work. Vouchers are a future upgrade; the site currently says “enquire”.
